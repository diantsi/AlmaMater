## AlmaMater Value Proposition Canvas

Research and analysis of the identified user profiles highlighted several recurring needs:

- **Students:** need a centralized way to discover relevant events, projects, and opportunities without monitoring multiple channels.
- **Project Initiators:** need an easier way to find reliable teammates with relevant skills and interests.
- **Student Organizations:** need better tools to promote activities, reach new audiences, and manage participants.
- **Faculty & Staff:** need an effective channel to publish academic opportunities and reach relevant students.
- **Common patterns:** scattered information, limited visibility, inefficient matching, fragmented registration tools, and difficulty keeping track of activities.

These findings were used as the basis for the Value Proposition Canvas below.

## Value Proposition Canvas

| **User Profile** | **Jobs** | **Pains** | **Gains** | **Solution** |
