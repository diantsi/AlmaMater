## User Story Mapping

This section turns the pains and the Value Proposition Canvas (VPC) into a pool of draft user stories and arranges them along the six steps of the happy-path user journey (see `raw_research_output/p3/3.1_user_journey.md`). The journey follows the head of a student organisation publishing a lecture event. Stories follow the organiser's steps, so most use the organiser role. Only step 6 adds *student* stories, because that step is where the published listing starts to serve students (a persona already defined in the README).

The pool is a raw brainstorm. It is not filtered, ranked, estimated or sliced into releases, and it includes small, ambitious and nice-to-have ideas.

**Journey steps:** 1. Sign in → 2. Start "Create event" → 3. Fill in event details → 4. Choose participation method and channels → 5. Preview, validate and publish → 6. Success

**Abbreviations used in the "Related Pain / VPC Item" column:**
*Org* = Student Organizations & Initiative Organizers · *Int* = Students Integrating into University Life · *Dev* = Development-Oriented Opportunity Seekers · *R#* = risk from the Critical Analysis table.

**Placement notes** (each story is placed once, in its most natural step):
- US-15 (topic and faculty pickers) also affects step 6: the same values power the student filters in US-50.
- US-21 (deadline) is entered in step 3, but US-30 (reminder posts) in step 4 relies on it.
- US-28, US-29 and US-30 (channel and timing choices) are in step 4. Step 5 previews their result (US-34, US-35).
- US-37 and US-38 (moderation) are in step 5, though a rejection also affects step 6 (the event doesn't go live).
- US-42 and US-45 touch distribution (step 4) but are placed in step 6, where their outcome is seen.
- US-44 (edit or cancel) is the moment organisers return to step 3 and reuse the same form.
- US-49 to US-54 are student stories and sit in step 6 because that is where the published listing meets its student audience. The journey itself covers only the organiser's actions.
